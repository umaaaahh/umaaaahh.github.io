💖 UMA BRIDGES 💖

Uma Bridges is my name and designing cool websites is my game.

I got my first taste of the creative world interning at Vast Yonder, assisting with site design for events from Yonder Festival to Brisbane Street Art Festival. From there I developed the rough concept for Squ1d 1nc while an artist in residence at Superordinary Brisbane.

Finally making my way to RMIT Melbourne, I applied the creative skills I'd picked up along the way as I learnt new ones in UX/UI — which have transformed the scope of what Squ1d 1nc can be. But 1nc stands for Incorporated, and these skills have indeed been incorporated into my practice.

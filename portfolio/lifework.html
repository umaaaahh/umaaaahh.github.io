// LIFEWORK — INTERNAL MESSAGES

A dystopian Slack-clone critiquing AI surveillance, gig economy exploitation, and the erosion of worker autonomy.

LifeWork Internal Messages is a fictional corporate intranet that doubles as social commentary. Every channel, message, and notification has been designed to feel uncannily familiar — while gradually revealing a system built to surveil, score, and dispose of its workers. The UX is deliberately indistinguishable from real productivity software, making the horror feel mundane.

🧠 The Overthinker's Typing Pad

// THE OVERTHINKER'S TYPING PAD

A browser-based expressive writing tool that visualizes anxiety through interaction.

As you type, the interface resists—text fades, glitches, and unravels, mirroring how overthinking spirals into creative shutdown. The tool transforms the act of writing into a visceral experience of mental friction, where each keystroke battles against self-doubt and creative paralysis.

🤖 Patchwork Cyborg

// PATCHWORK CYBORG

A two-part interactive series critiquing post-humanism through the lens of a dystopian New Naarm.

Players survive as exploited gig workers for the Lifework Corporation, upgrading biotech body parts to boost stats and outlast endless bills. The game explores themes of labor exploitation, bodily autonomy, and the dehumanizing effects of late-stage capitalism through dark humor and strategic gameplay.
